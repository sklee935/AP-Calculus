AP Calculus AB • L04
Factoring and conjugates

A conjugate can reveal a factor hidden by a square root.
Use the difference of squares to remove the radical from the numerator.

1. Multiply by a form of 1, so nearby values stay unchanged.
2. Cancel the exposed factor only where it is nonzero.
3. Now substitution is valid in the rewritten nearby expression.

Remember: Multiply both numerator and denominator by the conjugate.
