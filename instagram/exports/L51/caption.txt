Exponential growth and decay

A constant relative growth rate leads to an exponential model.
The derivative remains proportional to the current amount, not to the initial amount alone.

1. The initial value fixes the multiplicative constant.
2. The positive coefficient produces exponential growth.
3. Use exponential and logarithm rules to evaluate exactly.

Remember: Exponential decay approaches zero in this positive model; it does not become negative.
