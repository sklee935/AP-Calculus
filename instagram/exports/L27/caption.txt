Linearization and error direction

A tangent line gives a nearby estimate and concavity tells its error direction.
Near x=16, use the tangent line to replace a difficult square root.

1. The tangent line needs the known height and slope.
2. Use the input change x−16, not x itself.
3. The graph is concave down, so this tangent estimate is too high.

Remember: The error direction depends on concavity, not on whether the slope is positive.

CHECK ANSWER — try card 8 first
9.6, an underestimate. L=9+6(x−3); the upward-concave graph lies above its tangent.
