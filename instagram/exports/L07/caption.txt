Infinite limits and limits at infinity

A point going to infinity differs from an input going to infinity.
For end behavior of a rational function, compare the highest-degree terms.

1. Divide numerator and denominator by x².
2. Lower-order contributions disappear as the input grows.
3. The graph approaches horizontal height 1.5.

Remember: A vertical asymptote describes behavior near a finite input, not far-away input behavior.
