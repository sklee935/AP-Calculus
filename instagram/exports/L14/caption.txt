Basic function derivatives

Different function families have different basic derivative rules.
Use the correct basic rule for each term, then add.

1. A logarithm differentiates to a reciprocal, not another logarithm.
2. Combine the three derivatives without changing their signs.
3. The original domain remains part of the answer.

Remember: The derivative of cosine is negative sine; the sine rule has a different sign.
