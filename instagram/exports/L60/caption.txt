The washer method and course review

A washer removes the inner disk; finish by checking geometry, bounds and units.
The cross-sectional area is outer disk area minus inner disk area.

1. Square each radius separately, then subtract.
2. The intersection inputs set the integration bounds.
3. Across calculus, connect the model, conditions, method and interpretation.

Remember: The washer area is π(R²−r²), not π(R−r)².

CHECK ANSWER — try card 8 first
8π/15. Now R=2−x² and r=2−x. Integrate π[(2−x²)²−(2−x)²] from 0 to 1.
