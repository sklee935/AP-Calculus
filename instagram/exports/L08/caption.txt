The intermediate value theorem

You can prove a solution exists without finding its exact location.
A continuous graph cannot jump over a height between its endpoint values.

1. Evaluate the endpoint values first.
2. The target height zero lies strictly between those values.
3. Continuity and the endpoint comparison justify the existence conclusion.

Remember: This theorem guarantees existence, not uniqueness.
