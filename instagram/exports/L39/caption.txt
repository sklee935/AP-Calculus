Trapezoidal approximation

Trapezoids join neighboring data heights and accommodate unequal interval widths.
The average of two endpoint rates gives a trapezoid’s mean height.

1. The first width is one hour.
2. The second width is two hours.
3. Adding rate times time produces a volume estimate.

Remember: Tabular data alone may not justify an overestimate or underestimate claim.

양 끝 변화율의 평균이 사다리꼴의 평균 높이입니다.
