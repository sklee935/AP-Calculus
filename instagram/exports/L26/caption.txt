AP Calculus AB • L26
Cones and ladders

Geometry supplies the relationship before calculus supplies the rate.
A fixed ladder length links both moving distances. Cone geometry similarly links radius and depth.

1. Differentiate the relationship with respect to time.
2. The top moves downward at 1.5 feet per second.
3. For a cone with height 8 and radius 4, eliminate r before differentiating.

Remember: Use the current water radius, not the full cone radius, in the volume formula.

CHECK ANSWER — try card 8 first
3 cm/s. V′=(πh²/4)h′; at h=2, 3π=πh′.

기하 관계식을 먼저 만들고 미분으로 변화율을 구합니다.
사다리의 고정 길이가 두 거리를 연결합니다. 원뿔에서는 닮음이 반지름과 깊이를 연결합니다.
