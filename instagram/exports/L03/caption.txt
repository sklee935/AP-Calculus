Limits from tables and graphs

An open point and a filled point answer different questions.

1. The left segment climbs toward the open point.
2. The right segment approaches the same height.

Remember: A finite table suggests a limit; the function or graph supplies the supporting structure.

두 선분은 높이 2로 갑니다. 따로 있는 채운 점이 함숫값 4입니다.

1. 왼쪽 선분은 빈 점을 향해 올라갑니다.
