AP Calculus AB • L17
The chain rule

An outer power contains an inner function. Both layers contribute to the derivative.
Differentiate the outside, keep the inside, then multiply by the inside derivative.

1. The inside is not just x, so its derivative is essential.
2. Multiply the outer derivative by the inner derivative.
3. Substitute only after completing both layers.

Remember: Leaving out the inner derivative loses the chain rule.

CHECK ANSWER — try card 8 first
2x cos(x²). The outer sine gives cosine, and the inner x² gives 2x.

안을 유지하며 바깥을 미분하고, 안의 도함수를 곱합니다.
