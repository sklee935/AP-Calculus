Rates and units

A derivative becomes meaningful when its sign, input and units are stated.
A negative volume derivative describes water decreasing, not a negative amount of water.

1. Differentiate volume with respect to time.
2. At hour 3, volume is decreasing at 3 liters per hour.
3. The volume itself remains positive while its rate is negative.

Remember: A rate in L/h is different from an accumulated volume in L.
