The first derivative test

A zero derivative becomes a local extremum only when the behavior supports it.
Increasing then decreasing makes a local maximum; decreasing then increasing makes a local minimum.

1. The function turns from rising to falling.
2. The function turns from falling to rising.
3. State the sign change as the reason for each conclusion.

Remember: A derivative equal to zero without a sign change need not give an extremum.

CHECK ANSWER — try card 8 first
No. g′=3x² is positive on both sides of 0, so there is no sign change.
